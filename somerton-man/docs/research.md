# Research notes: the Somerton Man (the Tamám Shud case)

Source for every line: Wikipedia, "Tamam Shud case" (read November 2026), which cites the inquest record,
contemporary Adelaide newspapers, Gerry Feltus's *The Unknown Man* and the 2022 University of Adelaide
announcement. Where the article itself says "most accounts" or "claimed", the script hedges the same way.
