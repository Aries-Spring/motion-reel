commercialUsage corporation, allowRedistribution true, modification allowModificationRedistribution,
creditNotation unnecessary (credited here anyway).

The film never shows the original look: `sets/singer.js` restyles it at load time into an original
anime-girl Claude (recoloured hair, eyes and outfit textures, a drawn spark emblem and catchlights, a
spark hairpin, choker, pendant and mic built in three.js) and, for the twin under the floor, an inverted
palette of the same girl.

## The new base (in progress): `shino.vrm`

`shino.vrm` is **Sendagaya Shino**, one of the VRoid Studio beta preset avatars by pixiv Inc., released
under **CC0** (its VRM meta: licenseName CC0, allowedUserName Everyone, commercialUssageName Allow).
Copied unchanged from github.com/madjin/vrm-samples (`vroid/beta/Sendagaya_Shino.vrm`). As with the
first base, `sets/singer.js` restyles her at load time into Claude: the film never shows the original
navy-haired schoolgirl look.
